Sources & how density is calculated

Turn on each boundary type separately. Multiple types can be shown together; each has its own shading, labels and statistics.

Loading area boundaries...

Airbnb counts follow the listing filters across the full map, not just the current view. Licensed plots count unique parcels with a current city permit and follow the analysis-area setting independently of Airbnb filters. Areas with zero Airbnbs and zero licensed plots are hidden from the map, lists and downloads. Each boundary type counts independently; totals across types must not be added. Locations are approximate and counts represent listing IDs, not unique properties. Overlaps within a type remain unassigned.

Airbnb / residential plots = filtered entire-place listings (excluding likely hotels) ÷ actual residential GIS parcel count × 100. Each original city parcel ID is counted once. Residential codes include single-family, townhouses, duplexes, multifamily and condos; vacant residential parcels and common areas are excluded. Parcels are not dwelling units: one multifamily parcel can contain many homes. The city GIS has no dwelling-unit count field. Areas and city-edge scope use a point inside each original parcel. Percentages require the analysis-area limit.

Permit totals

Potential violations · screening stats

Mapped listings in the selected analysis area. Search, host and amenity filters do not change these statistics. These are screening estimates, not verified violations.

Over occupancy

Capacity: min(2 × bedrooms + 1, 8). When multiple-license listings are included, exactly two detected numbers use the existing provisional two-unit estimate: min(2 × bedrooms + 2, 16). Missing bedrooms can establish an excess above the absolute cap only; other unknown occupancy results are excluded from the percentage. Detected license numbers do not verify separate units.

Likely unlicensed · registration-number screening

A listing is flagged when its adjusted privacy circle misses every current-permit parcel. Likely hotels and unknown spatial results are excluded. The occupancy checkboxes above apply only to occupancy statistics.

Potential license revenue shortfall

Registration screening compares listing permit numbers with the CompassKC permit inventory.

This is a hypothetical comparison, not measured unpaid revenue. Listings and permits may not correspond one-to-one; some listings may not require a permit, multiple listings may share a property, and locations/statuses may be incomplete. It does not establish a violation or loss.

Important information

STVR Doorman is an independent community tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Kansas City, any government office, or any other agency.

Information and map classifications are estimates based on available and community-provided data. They are hypothetical screening results and do not prove that a law was violated, that a party occurred, or that a listing is unlicensed. Permit proximity, listing text, and automated estimates can be incomplete or incorrect. Please verify details with official sources and submit reports only when you have relevant evidence.

Data is current as of 9/30/2026. This site may or may not be updated. Permit statuses, listings, and other details can change after that date; verify current information with official sources.
