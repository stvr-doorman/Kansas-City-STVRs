Likely hotels

Orange markers identify likely hotels, resorts and apartment hotels from rental/property/room type, title, host and description. These listings have a separate inventory count in the footer (including records without coordinates) and are excluded from potentially unlicensed warnings, host warning counts, listings-minus-permits heatmaps and the revenue estimate. Popup evidence explains each match. Nearby hotel references and hotel-style comparisons are screened out. This automated category is not verified licensing status and may miss hotels with incomplete text.

Occupancy

Rule source: Kansas City Code § 56-803(2)h.7: adopted Ordinance 250965. The estimated capacity is min(2 × bedrooms + 1, 8). One bedroom allows 3 guests; two bedrooms allows 5; three bedrooms allows 7; four or more bedrooms allows 8 per dwelling unit. Marker numbers show advertised guests and, when above capacity, a second line such as “+4”.

Kansas City's rule counts the bedrooms being rented. The map uses advertised bedrooms, which are not independently verified permitted sleeping rooms. This compares advertised guest capacity with the general occupancy ceiling; it does not measure actual occupancy or verify lower property-specific safety limits. If bedrooms are missing, capacity is unknown; a listing above the absolute 8-person ceiling is still flagged for at least that excess. Missing guest counts are unknown, not zero.

When exactly two permit numbers are detected in listing text, the map uses a provisional two-unit estimate: bedrooms are split evenly, each unit receives +1 guest, and the combined cap is 16. Detected listing numbers are unverified and do not establish that two licensed units exist. Titles containing “two” add a popup caution only; they do not change capacity.

Registration-number screening

CompassKC supplies permit numbers, statuses, addresses, and parcel identifiers. Green plots are official KCMO parcel polygons with a current permit whose location ParcelNumber exactly matches the city's KIVAPIN. Parcel proximity and overlaps are not used to determine an Airbnb listing's license status. Yellow plots have expired permits; red plots have revoked or suspended permits. Where multiple records share a parcel, a current permit takes precedence, then revoked/suspended, then expired. A permit for one unit does not license every unit on a parcel. Blue circles show approximate Airbnb privacy radii only.

Green means the listing's registration number appears in CompassKC. Yellow means Potentially licensed: permit does not show up in license list. A missing number in this snapshot is not proof of an unlicensed listing. Red means likely unlicensed (no number in saved listing text or a non-current match) and/or above estimated capacity. Exempt and unassessed listings remain undetermined. Hotels are orange and excluded from licensing counts.

The footer counts unique listings in the loaded city dataset, including listings without coordinates, independently of display filters. Potentially licensed listings remain in that count even when over-capacity screening makes their marker red. The footer likely-unlicensed estimate is all loaded listings minus likely hotels minus current permit records, clamped to zero. It is separate from individual registration screening. Green connecting lines show direct current permit-number matches to parcels; Airbnb endpoints are approximate and do not verify address identity. The red density heatmap counts visible likely-unlicensed listings; it does not compare listings with geographically located permits.

Permit-number evidence

Extracted listing numbers are compared with the CompassKC permit inventory. Every detected number and direct match remains available in the listing card. A number match does not verify the listing's address or current licensing beyond the October 5, 2026 CompassKC snapshot. Numbers absent from CompassKC are potentially licensed and require verification.

Party house finder

🥳 marks listings with any selected amenity keyword category: 🩱 pool (excluding “pool table”), 👙 hot tub / hottub / jacuzzi, 🍻 the whole word “bar”, 🎱 pool table, 🎤 karaoke, 💍🤵🏻 bachelor-related terms, and 💍💐👰🏻‍♀️ bachelorette-related terms in listing text. Filters can require any or all selected categories and a minimum number of guests over estimated capacity. Symbols surround the marker when enabled. Keyword evidence is shown in the popup. Negations and references to nearby amenities can still match; these indicators do not prove parties, rule violations, or on-site amenities.

Downloads

Listing details and detected listing-number evidence are attributed to the linked Airbnb.com listing. They may be incomplete, out of date, or unverified. The map does not fetch live Airbnb pages or analyze listing photographs. Listings with limited details remain in the inventory.
